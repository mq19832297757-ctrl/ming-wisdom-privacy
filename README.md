# MING Privacy Policy

Three independently identified privacy policies for the MING Android application. The Samsung and ONE store pages are available in seven languages; the Huawei page retains its original five-language set.

- Samsung / Galaxy Store: <https://mq19832297757-ctrl.github.io/ming-wisdom-privacy/>
- Huawei / AppGallery: <https://mq19832297757-ctrl.github.io/ming-wisdom-privacy/huawei/>
- ONE store: <https://mq19832297757-ctrl.github.io/ming-wisdom-privacy/onestore/>

The Huawei page uses the Chinese legal company name only in Simplified Chinese. Traditional Chinese, English, French, and Korean use the registered English company name.
The ONE store page uses the same personal developer identity and data-handling terms as the Samsung edition, while identifying ONE store as the transaction provider.

The October 13, 2026 Samsung and ONE store updates describe saved home assessments and confirmed bearings, local calculation caches, deletion and manual backups.
